Orion Nebula

The Orion Nebula is a cloud of glowing gas about 24 light-years across, the nearest place where massive stars are forming. About 3,500 young stars crowd its centre. The biggest, θ¹ Orionis C, has 33 times the Sun's mass. Its ultraviolet light strips electrons from the hydrogen, which makes the gas glow. That glowing layer covers the near face of a much larger dark cloud. The star's wind has also blown out a hollow about 13 light-years wide.
