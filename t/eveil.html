Eastern Veil Nebula

NGC 6992 · NGC 6995 · IC 1340 · Caldwell 33 · Sh2-103
  • In the constellation Cygnus · 2,400 light-years from Earth
  • 3.8 hours · 46 × 300 s · 4 nights · Oct 17 – Nov 12, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro + ASI2600MM Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII
  • Annotated1080p 1440p
  • Framed and titled1080p 1440p

The Veil is the wreck of a star that exploded about 20,000 years ago. Its blast wave has since swept out a shell about 120 light-years wide. This eastern arc glows where the wave runs into clouds of denser gas and slows down. The collision heats the gas until it shines: blue from oxygen just behind the shock, gold from hydrogen as the gas cools.
