Monkey Head Nebula

NGC 2174 · NGC 2175 · Sh2-252 · LBN 854 · Ced 67
  • In the constellation Orion · 6,300 light-years from Earth
  • 1.7 hours · 20 × 300 s · 2 nights · Oct 24 – Nov 12, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro + ASI2600MM Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII

The Monkey Head Nebula is a cloud of hydrogen about 60 light-years across. Most of its light comes from one star near the centre, HD 42088, which formed 2 to 4 million years ago. The star's ultraviolet light strips electrons from the hydrogen, which makes it glow. The heated gas expands and squeezes the colder cloud along the top edge into a dense ridge. The Spitzer telescope found 577 young stars here, most of them on that side.
