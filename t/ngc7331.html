NGC 7331 and Stephan's Quintet

NGC 7331 is a spiral galaxy 45 million light-years away and about 140,000 light-years across. The small galaxies just below it look like its companions. They are not; they lie six to nine times farther away. To the right is Stephan's Quintet, the first tight group of galaxies ever discovered, in 1877. Four of its galaxies are colliding, 290 million light-years away. The fifth, bluer one is in the foreground, near NGC 7331's distance.
