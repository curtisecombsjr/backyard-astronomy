The Pleiades, or Seven Sisters, is a cluster of more than a thousand young stars, 444 light-years away. They formed together about 100 million years ago. The blue glow is not left over from their birth: the cluster is drifting through an unrelated cloud of dust, and the dust reflects the hot blue starlight. The fine parallel streaks show the dust being combed out as the stars pass through it.
