Andromeda Galaxy

M31 · NGC 224 · M32 · NGC 221 · M110 · NGC 205 · NGC 206
  • In the constellation Andromeda · 2.5 million light-years from Earth
  • 3.1 hours · 83 × 120 s + 30 × 10 s + 196 × 5 s · 3 nights · Sep 13 – Nov 7, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter

The Andromeda Galaxy is a spiral of about a trillion stars, 2.5 million light-years away. Its disc is about 130,000 light-years across. We see it nearly edge-on, so the round disc looks like a long oval. The yellow centre glows with old stars. Young blue stars line the spiral arms, and dark lanes of dust block the light behind them. Andromeda's gravity is pulling stars away from the two small galaxies beside it.
