M15 · NGC 7078

  • 1.1 hours · 122 × 30 s + 20 × 10 s · 1 night · Nov 7, 2026

M15 is a ball of half a million Suns' worth of stars, all born nearly 13 billion years ago, when the Milky Way was young. Close passes between stars have slowly sorted them: heavy stars sank toward the middle while light ones were flung outward. The core collapsed under this, packing over 100,000 Suns' worth of stars into each cubic light-year at the center. The orange stars are red giants, old stars that swelled and cooled as their fuel ran low.
