Cocoon Nebula

IC 5146 · Sh2-125 · Caldwell 19
  • In the constellation Cygnus · 2,500 light-years from Earth
  • 1.4 hours · 82 × 60 s · 1 night · Nov 10, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter

The Cocoon is a cloud of glowing hydrogen about 9 light-years across. It sits at the tip of a long lane of dark dust, where about 150 stars formed within the last million years or so. The heaviest, 15 times the Sun's mass, shines with ultraviolet light that strips electrons from the hydrogen, which makes it glow red. Dust around the edge scatters the same starlight, and dust scatters blue light best, so the rim looks blue.
