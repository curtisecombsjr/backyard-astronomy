Western Veil Nebula

NGC 6960 · Caldwell 34 · Sh2-103
  • In the constellation Cygnus · 2,400 light-years from Earth
  • 2.8 hours · 34 × 300 s · 3 nights · Oct 19 – 25, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII
  • Annotated1080p 1440p
  • Framed and titled1080p 1440p

The Witch's Broom is the western edge of the Veil, the shell of a star that exploded about 20,000 years ago. The shell is still spreading outward. Here its blast wave is plowing into a large cloud of cold gas. The impact squeezes and heats the gas until it glows: blue from oxygen right behind the shock, gold from hydrogen as the gas cools.
