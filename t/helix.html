Helix Nebula

NGC 7293 · Caldwell 63 · WD 2226-210 · HD 213055
  • In the constellation Aquarius · 650 light-years from Earth
  • 36 minutes · 432 × 5 s · 2 nights · Sep 25 – 28, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter

The Helix is the outer layers of a dying star, cast off over the last 11,000 years. What remains of the star sits at the centre: a white dwarf, its bare core, hotter than 100,000 °C. Its ultraviolet light strips electrons from the gas, which makes it glow. Oxygen needs the most energetic light to glow, so it shines blue close to the star. Hydrogen glows gold farther out, in a ring about 3 light-years across.
