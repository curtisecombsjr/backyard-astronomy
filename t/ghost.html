Ghost of Cassiopeia

IC 63 · Ced 4b · LBN 622 · IC 59 · Sh2-185 · Gamma Cassiopeiae
  • In the constellation Cassiopeia · 550 light-years from Earth
  • 0.8 hours · 9 × 300 s · 1 night · Oct 25, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII
  • Annotated1080p 1440p
  • Framed1080p 1440p

The Ghost is a small cloud of gas and dust about 550 light-years away. It lies about 8 light-years from Gamma Cassiopeiae, the middle star of Cassiopeia's W. That star has 15 times the Sun's mass. Its ultraviolet light strips electrons from the cloud's hydrogen, which makes it glow. The same light is slowly boiling the cloud away, so its sharpest edge faces the star. Hydrogen glows deep red; this picture shows it in blue.
