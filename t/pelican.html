Pelican Nebula

IC 5070 · IC 5067 · Sh2-117
  • In the constellation Cygnus · 2,600 light-years from Earth
  • 8.0 hours · 96 × 300 s · 4 nights · Oct 15 – Nov 11, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro + ASI2600MM Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII

The Pelican Nebula is a cloud of glowing hydrogen about 45 light-years across. It is one half of a larger glowing cloud. A lane of dark dust in front hides the middle and splits it from the North America Nebula (not pictured here). Both halves are lit by one massive star hidden behind that dust, the Bajamar star (not pictured here). The bright curved rim of the Pelican's head is where its light is eating into colder gas.
