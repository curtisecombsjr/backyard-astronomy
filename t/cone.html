Cone Nebula

NGC 2264 · Sh2-273 · Christmas Tree Cluster · Fox Fur Nebula · S Monocerotis
  • In the constellation Monoceros · 2,300 light-years from Earth
  • 1.2 hours · 15 × 300 s · 2 nights · Oct 23 – 24, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII
  • Annotated1080p 1440p
  • Framed and titled1080p 1440p

NGC 2264 is a cloud of gas and dust where up to 1,000 stars formed about 3 million years ago: the Christmas Tree Cluster. Its brightest member, S Monocerotis, is a pair of massive stars you can see without a telescope. Their ultraviolet light makes the gas glow and slowly wears the cloud away. The densest gas lasts longest and stands out as the dark Cone, its tip facing S Mon.
