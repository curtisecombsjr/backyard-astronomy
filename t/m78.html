Monkey Head Nebula

Messier 78

M78 · NGC 2068 · NGC 2071 · HH 24
  • In the constellation Orion · 1,300 light-years from Earth
  • 2.8 hours · 165 × 60 s · 2 nights · Nov 9 – 10, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter

M78 is a cloud of dust about 4 light-years across, lit from inside by a pair of young stars. Their light bounces off the dust, and dust scatters blue light best, so M78 shines blue. The stars around it formed about 2 million years ago. Four in five still have the dusty disks that planets form in. The red glow at the right is Barnard's Loop, the rim of a vast hollow cleared by Orion's stars. M78's cloud lies along the same rim.
