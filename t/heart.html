Heart Nebula

IC 1805 · Sh2-190
  • In the constellation Cassiopeia · 6,800 light-years from Earth
  • 1.3 hours · 16 × 300 s · 1 night · Oct 11, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII

The Heart Nebula is a cloud of hydrogen more than 200 light-years across. At its centre is Melotte 15, a cluster born a few million years ago. It holds nine stars more than 15 times the Sun's mass. Their ultraviolet light makes the gas glow. Hydrogen shines red throughout, but oxygen needs harsher light, so its blue-green glow fills the middle, near the hottest stars.
