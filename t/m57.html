Ring Nebula

M57 · NGC 6720 · IC 1296 · HD 175577

  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter

The Ring Nebula is the outer layers of a dying star, blown off roughly 4,500 years ago. We look at it almost straight down its axis, so a thick, hollow doughnut of gas looks like a ring about one light-year across. The star's bare core, at 120,000 °C, lights the gas with ultraviolet. Oxygen glows teal close to the star, where that light is most energetic. Hydrogen and nitrogen glow red at the outer edge.
