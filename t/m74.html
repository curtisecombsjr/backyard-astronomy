Phantom Galaxy

M74 · NGC 628 · UGC 1149 · PGC 5974
  • In the constellation Pisces · 32 million light-years from Earth
  • 3.3 hours · 98 × 120 s · 2 nights · Nov 9 – 10, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter

M74 is a spiral galaxy 32 million light-years away and nearly 100,000 light-years across, about the size of our Milky Way. We see it almost exactly face-on, so its two arms lie open. The yellow centre is old stars. The blue arms are young, hot stars, and the faint pink specks are hydrogen clouds that newborn massive stars make glow. Massive stars burn out within a few million years, and at least three in M74 have exploded since 2002.
