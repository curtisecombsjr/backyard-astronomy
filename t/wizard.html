Wizard Nebula

NGC 7380 · Sh2-142
  • In the constellation Cepheus · 8,500 light-years from Earth
  • 12.8 hours · 153 × 300 s · 8 nights · Oct 17 – Nov 14, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro + ASI2600MM Pro · L-Ultimate + 3 nm Hα / OIII

The Wizard Nebula is a cloud of glowing hydrogen about 70 light-years across. Most of its light comes from DH Cephei, which is really two massive stars, each about 30 times the Sun's mass. Their ultraviolet light pushes into the cloud's edges and squeezes the gas there. Dense pockets of squeezed gas collapse into new stars. The stars forming this way are less than a million years old.
