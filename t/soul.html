Soul Nebula

IC 1848 · Sh2-199
  • In the constellation Cassiopeia · 7,500 light-years from Earth
  • 5.2 hours · 62 × 300 s · 6 nights · Oct 17 – Nov 11, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro + ASI2600MM Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII
  • Annotated|1080p|1440p
  • Framed and titled|1080p|1440p

The Soul is a glowing hydrogen cloud about 300 light-years long. A few million years ago, a cluster of massive stars formed inside it, some of them 20 times the Sun's mass. Their intense light and the gas they blow off pushed the cloud outward, clearing two hollow cavities.
