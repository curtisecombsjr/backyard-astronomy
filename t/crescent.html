Crescent Nebula

NGC 6888 · Caldwell 27 · Sh2-105
  • In the constellation Cygnus · 5,400 light-years from Earth
  • 12.1 hours · 145 × 300 s · 7 nights · Oct 11 – Nov 12, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro + ASI2600MM Pro · L-Ultimate Hα/OIII

The Crescent is a bubble blown by one massive, dying star, WR 136. Long ago the star swelled into a red supergiant and shed its outer layers. Now stripped to its hot core, it blows a wind of about 1,000 miles a second. That wind is sweeping the old layers into this shell, about 25 light-years across. The gas trapped inside is over a million degrees.
