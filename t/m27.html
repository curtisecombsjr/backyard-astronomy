Dumbbell Nebula

M27 · NGC 6853
  • In the constellation Vulpecula · 1,300 light-years from Earth
  • 30 minutes · 30 × 60 s · 1 night · Nov 7, 2026
  • Askar 120 · ASI2600MC Pro · no filter
  • Annotated1080p 1440p
  • Framed and titled1080p 1440p

The Dumbbell Nebula is the shell of gas thrown off by a dying star, about 3 light-years across. Charles Messier found it in 1764, the first of these dying-star shells ever seen. About 10,000 years ago the star puffed off its outer layers, leaving its hot bare core. That core's ultraviolet light makes the gas glow: oxygen shines blue-green near the star, while hydrogen and nitrogen glow red at the edges, where the starlight is used up.
